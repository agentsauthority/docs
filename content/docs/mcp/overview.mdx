Imagine a user tells your AI agent: "Order me the cheapest standing desk under $400." With Agents Authority, your agent can find it, check stock, and complete the purchase — without leaving the conversation.

## What your agent gets

- **Shop any connected store** — search products, filter by price, compare options, check real-time stock
- **Pay from a wallet** — your agent draws from a pre-funded wallet; no card details passed in code
- **Respect spending rules** — set daily limits, block merchants, or require approval above a threshold
- **Make autonomous purchases** — create a mandate once; your agent can reorder without asking each time

## How a purchase works

1. **Find the store** — your agent looks up the merchant and gets a shopping connection
2. **Browse products** — search by keyword, category, or price range; filter to in-stock only
3. **Start a checkout** — spending rules are checked automatically; if anything is out of bounds, it stops here
4. **Complete the purchase** — payment is authorized, the order is placed, and a receipt is recorded

Your agent does not need to handle payment credentials or call policy tools manually. Governance runs automatically in the background.
