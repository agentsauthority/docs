---
title: "Settings Guide"
description: "Every setting in the Agents Authority plugin and what it controls."
---

Access all settings at **WordPress Admin → Agents Authority → Settings**.

## Connect or disconnect

**API Key** — paste your key from the AgentsAuthority dashboard here. After connecting, the field shows your masked key.

**Connect** — links your store to AgentsAuthority. Products sync automatically.

**Disconnect** — unlinks your store and removes it from AI agent discovery. Your WooCommerce store continues working normally.

**Connection status** shows whether your store is live:
- Green badge: connected, with your store name and last sync time
- Yellow badge: not connected, with the API key form

## Control what agents can find

These settings are under **Agent Visibility**. They only affect AI agent discovery — your regular store and customers are unaffected.

**In-stock only** — when on, agents only see products you currently have in stock. Out-of-stock products are hidden.

**Minimum quantity** — hide products from agents when stock falls below a number you set. Useful to hold back a few units for direct customers. Set to 0 to disable.

**Hidden categories** — select any product categories to hide entirely from agent discovery.

**Per-product visibility** — on any product's edit page, under the **General** tab, check **Hide from AI Agents** to exclude that specific product.

## Monitor activity

**Sync status** — shows the last sync time and how many products are currently synced.

**Sync Products Now** — triggers an immediate full sync of all your published products.

**Auto-sync interval** — controls how often the plugin automatically runs a full sync in the background (default: every hour). Options: 15 minutes, hourly, every 6 hours, daily, or manual only.

**Store stats** — live counts of your published products, categories, and on-sale items.

## MCP endpoint

After connecting, the **MCP Server** card shows your store's MCP endpoint URL. You can share this URL with developers or add it to MCP-compatible tools like Claude Desktop or Cursor. A copy button is next to the URL.

## Debug logs

Enable detailed logging only when troubleshooting. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/plugins/woocommerce/troubleshooting) for instructions.
