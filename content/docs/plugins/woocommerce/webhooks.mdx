---
title: "AI Orders in WooCommerce"
description: "What AI agent purchases look like in your WooCommerce store and how to handle them."
---

When an AI agent completes a purchase in your store, it goes through your standard WooCommerce checkout — the same flow any customer uses. You don't need a separate process to handle AI-originated orders.

## Where to find AI agent orders

Go to **WooCommerce → Orders**. AI-originated orders appear in the same list as all your other orders.

Orders placed via AI agents include an order note identifying the source. Look for a note like "Order placed via AgentsAuthority" in the order detail view.

## Fulfillment

Fulfill AI agent orders exactly as you would any other order:
- Pick, pack, and ship as normal
- Mark the order as **Processing** then **Completed** when shipped
- All your existing shipping rules, tax settings, and fulfillment workflows apply

## Refunds

Process refunds for AI agent orders the same way you process any WooCommerce refund. Go to the order, click **Refund**, and follow your standard process.

## Payments

AI agent orders use the same payment methods your store accepts. If you accept Stripe or PayPal, those gateways handle AI agent transactions the same way they handle direct customer transactions.
