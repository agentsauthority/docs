When you connect your store, AgentsAuthority creates a storefront profile that AI agents can read. Think of it like a Google Business Profile, but built specifically for AI agents.

## What the profile includes

- Your store name and the categories you sell in
- Your accepted payment methods
- Whether you offer shipping
- Whether you accept coupons or discount codes
- Your privacy policy, refund policy, and terms of service links

The plugin builds this profile automatically from your WooCommerce configuration. You don't fill it out — it's generated from your existing settings.

## When it updates

The profile updates automatically when you make changes to your WooCommerce setup:

- Add a new payment gateway → agents see it within the hour
- Create a shipping zone → agents know you offer fulfillment
- Enable or disable coupons → reflected in the next update

You don't need to do anything to keep it current.

## If an agent can't find your store

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Wait 5 minutes
    After first connecting, allow up to 5 minutes for your store to become discoverable.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check your connection
    Go to **Agents Authority → Settings** and confirm the status shows a green **Connected** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Reconnect if needed
    If the badge shows yellow, click **Connect** and enter your API key again.
  </Step>
</Steps>
