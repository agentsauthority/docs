## Get your API key

Log in to your [AgentsAuthority dashboard](https://agentsauthority.ai) and go to **Settings → API Keys**. Copy your key.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open the plugin settings
    In WordPress admin, go to **Agents Authority → Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Paste your API key
    Paste your key into the **API Key** field and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Wait for sync
    A progress indicator appears while your products sync. This takes about 30 seconds for most stores.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Done
    The settings page shows a green **Connected** badge with your store name and the number of products synced. Your store is live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
The plugin configures everything automatically. You don't need to touch WooCommerce settings or set up API keys manually.
</Callout>

## Disconnect

To disconnect, click **Disconnect** in the plugin settings. Your store is removed from AI agent discovery immediately. Your WooCommerce store continues to work normally for regular customers.

<Callout>
Disconnecting does not delete your store data on AgentsAuthority. To permanently remove your store, delete it from your AgentsAuthority dashboard after disconnecting.
</Callout>
