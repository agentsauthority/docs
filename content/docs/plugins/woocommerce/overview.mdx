The moment you install this plugin, your products become discoverable by AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor. AI agents can browse your catalog, check stock, and guide customers through purchase — all without you changing anything about how your store works.

## What AI agents can do in your store

- **Find products** — search by name, category, keyword, or price range
- **Check availability** — real-time stock status before recommending a product
- **Complete purchases** — guide customers through checkout using your existing payment methods
- **Respect your rules** — agents only see what you allow (you control visibility per product and per category)

## What this means for your business

Your store opens to an entirely new customer channel. When someone asks an AI assistant to "find me a leather wallet under $60," your products can appear in that answer — not just in Google search results. No additional work on your end. Your existing WooCommerce setup, pricing, and fulfillment stay exactly as they are.

## Requirements

- WordPress 6.0 or higher
- WooCommerce 7.0 or higher (8.0+ recommended)
- PHP 7.4 or higher
- An AgentsAuthority account — [create one free](https://agentsauthority.ai)
