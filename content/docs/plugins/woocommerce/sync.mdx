## How sync works

Your products stay current automatically. When you save a product in WooCommerce — a price change, stock update, new description — AI agents see the updated version within seconds.

You don't need to do anything to keep it current.

## Sync everything now

If you want to force an immediate full update, go to **Agents Authority → Settings** and click **Sync Products Now**. The settings page shows the last sync time and how many products are synced.

## Hide a product from AI agents

You can exclude any product from agent discovery without affecting its visibility to regular customers.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Edit the product
    Open the product in WooCommerce and go to the **General** tab in Product data.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check the hide option
    Check **Hide from AI Agents**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Update**. The product is hidden from agents immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The product remains live on your store for human shoppers.

## Control what agents see across your whole catalog

Go to **Agents Authority → Settings → Agent Visibility** to set catalog-wide rules:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| In-stock only | Agents only see products that are currently in stock |
| Minimum quantity | Hide products when stock falls below a set number — useful for reserving last units |
| Hidden categories | All products in selected categories are hidden from agents |

These settings only affect agent discovery. Your store and your regular customers are unaffected.
